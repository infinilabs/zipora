## 2. `S41-R1..R3` — Stage 14 Carry-Over Closure

### 2.1 `S41-R1`: Best-of-6 (`--confirm-retries 5`) Detection Sensitivity Disclosure (`docs/review/e1.md:152-154`)
- Updated `docs/review/e1.md` §4.2 (`docs/review/e1.md:152-154`) to disclose the statistical effect of `--confirm-retries 5`:
  - Passing a benchmark if any of up to `1 + 5 = 6` attempts (`1` initial full-suite pass + up to `5` isolated `--filter` retries) falls within the `+9.0%` (`min_ns_per_op`) threshold takes the minimum across up to 6 runs, which compresses measured deltas by `~2–3%` on DRAM-bandwidth-bound benchmarks with natural shared-host run-to-run variance (`~2–4%`).
  - In the reviewer's `S40` real-regression probe (`Rank9::rank1_fast` mutated with a tunable slowdown), a true **`+13.5%`** slowdown (`49.9 ns` vs `44.1–44.9 ns` unmutated control) measured **`+10.3%`** on the best of 6 attempts and failed the `+9.0%` gate (`exit 1` after 5 retries), whereas a true regression of roughly **`+10–12%`** on a memory-bandwidth-bound benchmark can slip past `+9.0%` on the best of 6 attempts. Tight-loop CPU-bound benchmarks with `5.0%` thresholds and `< 0.5%` spread remain sensitive down to `~+5.5%`.

### 2.2 `S41-R2`: Recorded Benchmark Binary Path & SHA-256 Verification in Retry Gate (`benches/common/mod.rs:887-1017`, `scripts/bench_compare.py:155-225`)

If the binary is missing or its SHA-256 does not match the initial pass, `run_isolated_retry` aborts the retry immediately.

## 4. `E3` — Standing Table Across All 176 Same-Run Competitor Comparisons

### 4.1 Definition of the `E3` Bar
- **Primary metric**: operation latency/throughput (`min_ns_per_op` in `ns/op`, `ns/elem`, `ns/byte`, `ns/int`, `ns/call`, or `ns/query`, where lower is better).
- **Secondary metric**: memory footprint or compressed size (`% of raw bitvector`, `bits/elem`, `bytes/key`, `bytes/entry`, `bits/byte`, `bits/int`, `bytes/elem`, `bytes/byte`, `aux bytes`, or `bytes/posting`, where lower is better).
- **`E3` Leading Criterion (`LEADING`)**:
  1. **Primary metric**: Zipora is at or above the competitor within `±3%` (`primary_speedup_ratio = competitor_min / zipora_min >= 0.97`, i.e., `primary_delta_pct <= +3.0%`).
  2. **Secondary metric**: Zipora is no more than `10%` worse than the competitor on space/compression (`secondary_delta_pct <= +10.0%`).
- **Verdict Classifications in `same_run_comparisons`**:
  - **`LEADING`**: Passes both (1) `primary_speedup_ratio >= 0.97` and (2) `secondary_delta_pct <= +10.0%`.
  - **`GAP (E3.x — secondary > +10%)`**: Zipora beats or ties the competitor on primary latency (`primary_speedup_ratio >= 0.97`), but uses `> 10%` more space/bits on the secondary metric.
  - **`GAP (E3.x)`**: Zipora trails the competitor by `> 3%` on primary latency (`primary_speedup_ratio < 0.97`).
