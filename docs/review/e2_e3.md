### 2.6 `S43-R1..R4`: Post-Reboot Re-Baseline, Allocator Arena Equalization Across `--filter` Retries, & `core_bench` `simd_strstr_64kib` Layout Alignment

## 4. `E3` — Standing Table Across All 197 Same-Run Competitor Comparisons

### 4.1 Definition of the `E3` Bar & Benchmark Allocator Policy (`S44-R1` / `S45-R3` Disclosure)
- **Workload Invariant (`S42-R1`)**: Every comparison in `competitor_bench.json` (`196` rows) and `e2e_search.json` (`1` row) runs **1 instance vs 1 instance (`1 vs 1`)** on the identical dataset and query stream with zero per-side heap spacers.
- **Primary metric**: operation latency (`min_ns_per_op` in `ns/op`, `ns/elem`, `ns/byte`, `ns/int`, `ns/call`, or `ns/query`, lower is better).
- **Secondary metric**: memory footprint or compressed size (`% of raw bitvector`, `bits/elem`, `bytes/key`, `bytes/entry`, `bits/byte`, `bits/int`, `bytes/elem`, `bytes/byte`, `aux bytes`, or `bytes/posting`, lower is better).
- **`LEADING`**: `primary_delta_pct <= +3.0%` (`primary_speedup_ratio >= 0.97`) **and** `secondary_delta_pct <= +10.0%`.
- **`GAP`**: Either `primary_delta_pct > +3.0%` (slower on primary) or `secondary_delta_pct > +10.0%` (faster/tied on primary, `> +10%` space/bits on secondary).
- **Benchmark Allocator Policy Condition (`S44-R1` / `S45-R3`)**:
  - All three benchmark binaries (`core_bench`, `competitor_bench`, `e2e_search_bench`) invoke `init_bench_allocator_policy()` (`benches/common/datasets.rs:530-545`) at startup, setting `libc::mallopt(libc::M_MMAP_MAX, 0)` and `libc::mallopt(libc::M_TRIM_THRESHOLD, -1)` so multi-megabyte allocations (`> 32 MiB`) use reusable `brk` heap pages instead of per-trial kernel `mmap()`/`munmap()` (`~70,000` minor page faults per trial) or per-iteration `systrim()` (`sbrk(-delta)`), with explicit `libc::malloc_trim(0)` executed between benchmarks.
  - **Impact on Large-Buffer Construction Rows & Standing (`S45-R3`)**: Disabling glibc's `32 MiB` `mmap` threshold and automatic `systrim` speeds up benchmarks that repeatedly allocate and drop `> 32 MiB` buffers across trials by **`25%–45%`** compared to default glibc `malloc` (`3f6189d`):
    - `tries/dat/url_paths/build_sorted`: `539.729 -> 381.947 ns/key` (`-29.2%`)
    - `tries/dat/random_16b/build_sorted`: `367.269 -> 274.244 ns/key` (`-25.3%`)
    - `tries/dat/shared_prefix/build_sorted`: `353.816 -> 196.212 ns/key` (`-44.5%`), and `tries/radix_trie/shared_prefix/build`: `290.517 -> 204.331 ns/key` (`-29.7%`)
    - `radix_sort/voracious/u32`: `16.820 -> 10.099 ns/elem` (`-39.3%`), `voracious/u64`: `26.982 -> 10.680 ns/elem` (`-30.7%`), `voracious/kv_u64_u64`: `51.832 -> 14.825 ns/elem` (`-32.2%`)
    - `suffix_array/construct`: `165.410 -> 77.155 ns/byte` (`-7.8%`)
  - **Historical Stage 15 Verdict Flip Disclosed & Resolved in Stage 16 (`E4(e)` `21d6e06`)**: At Stage 15 (`b4148fc`), `DoubleArrayTrie::build_from_sorted` on `shared_prefix` was `196.212 ns/key` (`1.041x LEADING` vs `radix_trie` `204.331 ns/key`) under `init_bench_allocator_policy()`, vs `353.816 ns/key` (`0.821x GAP` vs `290.517 ns/key`) under default glibc `malloc` (`3f6189d`). With Stage 16 `E4(e)` (`21d6e06`) replacing the incremental `insert` loop with top-down zero-relocation range partitioning, `tries/dat/shared_prefix/build_sorted` now runs at **`59.313 ns/key`** (`3.495x LEADING` vs `radix_trie` `207.289 ns/key`, `2.264x LEADING` vs `qp_trie` `134.298 ns/key`, and `1.587x` faster than `fst` `94.138 ns/key`), leading under both allocator policies.
